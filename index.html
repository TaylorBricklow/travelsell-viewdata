TravelSell Viewdata · a trip down memory lane

Type on your keyboard or tap the keys, and tap the coloured 50 to 54 labels on the screen. Enter or # sends what you have typed, * clears it, and *02 then Enter logs out. On the search form the cursor blinks in the box you are filling: type BHX and it moves on for EMA, then MAN; # moves down to the next box and the up arrow goes back. 54 opens the A-Z directory, where the up and down arrows and Enter pick one. All prices, times, hotels and ships are made up. Created by Jamie Bricklow.
